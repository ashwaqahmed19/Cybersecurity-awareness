# Cybersecurity Awareness

Hey there
Welcome to this little corner of GitHub where we talk about something super important: **staying safe online**.  
---

## Why this repo exists
We live in a world where clicking the wrong link or reusing the same password can cause big problems. Cybersecurity isn’t just for IT pros — it’s for everyone. This repo is here to make the topic less scary and more practical. Think of it as your friendly guide to digital safety.

---

## What you’ll find inside
- **Guides** → Easy-to-follow notes on things like strong passwords, spotting phishing emails, and safe browsing.  
- **Examples** → Real-life scenarios (because stories stick better than rules).  
- **Resources** → Trusted links if you want to dive deeper.  
- **Tips.md** → Quick everyday checklist you can skim in under 2 minutes.  

---

## Topics we cover
- How to create passwords you’ll actually remember (without making hackers smile)  
- Why multi-factor authentication is worth the extra tap  
- Tricks scammers use in emails and texts  
- Safe habits when you’re browsing or shopping online  
- Protecting your devices and Wi-Fi at home  
- Sharing files without oversharing your data
